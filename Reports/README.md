\# Reports



This folder contains the major reports completed during the Security and Risk Management module of the MSc Cybersecurity programme at the University of Essex.



The reports demonstrate the application of recognised cybersecurity frameworks, risk assessment methodologies, governance principles, quantitative analysis, and professional report writing in both individual and collaborative assessments.



\## Contents



\- \*\*Assessment Report\*\* – Technical security assessment of a web application, including vulnerability identification, risk analysis, and mitigation recommendations.



\- \*\*Individual Project Executive Summary\*\* – Strategic organisational risk assessment using quantitative risk modelling and evidence-based decision making.



\- \*\*Pampered Pets Digitalisation Risk Assessment\*\* – Collaborative assessment evaluating organisational risks associated with digital transformation.



\- \*\*Team Risk Identification Report\*\* – Team-based identification, analysis, and prioritisation of cybersecurity risks with recommended controls.
